Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Alligator Landing collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the Alligator Landing Property Management System, available at alligatorlanding.com.

Information We Collect

  • Name and email address
  • Phone number (for SMS notifications)
  • Property activity data including task completions, work orders, observations, and hunt logs
  • Photos and videos uploaded in connection with property tasks and work orders

How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticating crew members and managing access
  • Sending SMS notifications for task assignments and due date reminders
  • Tracking property maintenance, observations, and hunting activity

SMS Notifications

Crew members who provide a phone number may receive SMS notifications. Message frequency varies. Standard message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Data is shared only with Supabase and Twilio solely to operate the App.
