Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Alligator Landing ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the Alligator Landing Property Management System ("the App"), available at alligatorlanding.com.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information from authorized crew members who are invited to use the App:

  • Name and email address
  • Phone number (for SMS notifications)
  • Property activity data including task completions, work orders, observations, and hunt logs
  • Photos and videos uploaded in connection with property tasks and work orders

How We Use Your Information

Information collected is used solely for the operation of the Alligator Landing private property management system, including:

  • Authenticating crew members and managing access
  • Sending SMS notifications for task assignments, work order assignments, and due date reminders
  • Tracking property maintenance, observations, and
