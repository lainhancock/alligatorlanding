Privacy Policy Last updated: September 16, 2026 This Privacy Policy describes how Alligator Landing ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the Alligator Landing Property Management System ("the App"), available at alligatorlanding.com. Information We Collect We collect the following information from authorized crew members who are invited to use the App: Name and email address Phone number (for SMS notifications) Property activity data including task completions, work orders, observations, and hunt logs Photos and videos uploaded in connection with property tasks and work orders How We Use Your Information Information collected is used solely for the operation of the Alligator Landing private property management system, including: Authenticating crew members and managing access Sending SMS notifications for task assignments, work order assignments, and due date reminders Tracking property maintenance, observations, and hunting activity Maintaining an audit log of property-related activities SMS Notifications Crew members who provide a phone number may receive SMS notifications related to their assigned tasks and work orders on the Alligator Landing property. Message frequency varies based on property activity. Standard message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to opt out of SMS notifications. We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Data Sharing We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Information is shared only with service providers necessary to operate the App, including Supabase (database and authentication) and Twilio (SMS delivery). These providers are bound by confidentiality obligations and may not use your information for any other purpose. Data Security We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS), secure authentication, and access controls to protect your information. Access to the App is by invitation only. Data Retention We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to operate the property management system. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting the property owner. Who Has Access The App is a private, invitation-only system. Only the property owner and authorized crew members have access. There is no public registration.
